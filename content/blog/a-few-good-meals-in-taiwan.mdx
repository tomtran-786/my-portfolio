Food has turned out to be one of the easiest ways to feel settled here.
Some meals are quiet wins, some are full surprises. These three stood out
enough to write about.

## Brunch on Wuhua Road

This one was from a local brunch shop on Wuhua Road, the kind of place
serving Western-style plates rather than the usual Taiwanese breakfast. I
went with a toasted sandwich, thick white bread, lettuce, tomato, and a
generous slab of meat tucked inside. Honestly, it was on the bland side. But
the ingredients were clearly good quality, and for NT$80 I wasn't about to
complain.

![A toasted sandwich with meat, lettuce, and tomato, wrapped in brown paper from a brunch shop on Wuhua Road](/blog/wuhua-road-brunch-sandwich.jpg)

_NT$80 for a simple, well-made sandwich. Not the most exciting bite, but a
solid one._

## A soup that earned a regular

Same day, different meal entirely. A bowl of clear seafood soup, clams,
shrimp, a few slices of meat, and a handful of greens, with thin noodles
hiding underneath. I wasn't expecting much walking in, and then the first
spoonful landed. Clean, fresh, exactly the kind of broth that makes you
want to go back before you've even finished the bowl. This one's already
on my list of places to return to.

![A bowl of clear seafood soup with clams, greens, and thin noodles, next to a bottle of iced tea](/blog/seafood-noodle-soup.jpg)

_Clams, greens, thin noodles, and a broth good enough to make me a
regular._

## Something warm and sweet after

To close out the day, a bowl of douhua (豆花), warm tofu pudding served
with peanuts and chewy taro and sweet potato balls. Mildly sweet, light,
and genuinely refreshing rather than heavy the way dessert soups back home
sometimes are. The toppings tasted fresh, not like they'd been sitting
around, and at NT$80 it felt like a proper reward for the day.

![A bowl of warm douhua dessert soup with peanuts and taro and sweet potato balls](/blog/douhua-dessert.jpg)

_Warm douhua with peanuts and taro balls. Light, not too sweet, and worth
every bit of the NT$80._

## The pattern so far

Three meals, three different feelings, and that seems to be how food in
Taiwan works for me right now. Some things are just fine. Some things stop
me mid-bite. Either way, I'm having a good time figuring out which is
which.
