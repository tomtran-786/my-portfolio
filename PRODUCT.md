# Product

Two pages, one person, one visual grammar.

## `/` Main portfolio

- **Who reads it:** recruiters and hiring managers for finance, investment analysis, and data roles; people Tom met at events who want to check his work.
- **Job of the page:** show real projects with evidence (decks, dashboards, GitHub), then make it easy to get in touch.
- **Primary action:** download the resume. **Secondary:** book a coffee chat.
- **Tone:** concrete and calm. Numbers come with context. No superlatives.
- **Language:** English.

## `/teaching` English teaching

- **Who reads it:** Vietnamese students (high school, university, working adults) and parents choosing an English tutor for IELTS, TOEIC, or speaking.
- **Job of the page:** show that Tom teaches real classes with real results, explain each course clearly (level, format, price), and get a free trial booked.
- **Primary action:** message Tom on Zalo to book the free 30-minute trial lesson. **Secondary:** email.
- **Tone:** warm, direct, personal. First person ("tôi"). No hype.
- **Language:** Vietnamese.

## Writing rules for both pages

- No em-dashes. Use commas, colons, or full stops.
- No "not X, but Y" slogans. State the useful fact directly.
- Section subtitles say something specific (who, what, where), never filler like "Where you can reach out to me".
- Sentence case for nav, buttons, labels, and headings.
